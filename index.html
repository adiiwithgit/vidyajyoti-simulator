Equations in use

P_solar = G · η · PF · η_mppt · Σ(Aᵢ · cos θᵢ)
G = 1361 W/m², PF = 0.85, η_mppt = 0.95
T = 2π √(a³ / μ),  μ = 398600 km³/s²
Eclipse if r·ŝ < 0 and |r − (r·ŝ)ŝ| < R_E
E_batt(t+dt) = E + (P_gen − P_load)·dt · η_chg
η_chg = η_dis = 0.95,  DoD = 1 − SoC

Attitude is nadir-pointing. Six body faces: four sides of 0.03 m² and two ends of 0.01 m². Orbit-average margin = (P_gen − P_load) / P_load over one full orbit.
